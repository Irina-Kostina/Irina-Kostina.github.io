MY STORY

From Finance to Code

My background in accounting taught me to think logically and spot patterns, but coding gave me the tools to build real solutions — not just analyse problems. What started as weekend experiments soon became the start of a career change I genuinely enjoy.

I care about clarity, collaboration, and creating experiences that feel simple and meaningful. I bring a mix of logic, attention to detail, and problem-solving from my past experience, and aim to make things clear — both for users and the people I work with.

These days, I bring that same analytical mindset into every project, along with curiosity and a love for learning. When I’m not coding, I’m probably out by the ocean, trying a new recipe, or behind the camera capturing small, beautiful things.
