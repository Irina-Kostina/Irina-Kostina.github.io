This blog post is a reflection on my core Personal Learning Plan and Bootcamp Learning Plan.

What is my long term goal or career pathway?

My long-term goal is to transition into a career in software development, find a great job and find the area in software development that suits me best. Possibly mobile app development or backend work. I want to become a skilled and reliable specialist in this field, ideally working in FinTech industry, because I have experience in finance and accounting. I’m aiming for a role that allows remote or flexible work so I can have a better balance in life.

Personally, I’d love to buy a house somewhere up north from Auckland, get a dog, work remotely, and have the freedom to travel more often.

A description of my strengths and limitations when it comes to learning

Strengths:

— Self-disciplined and well-organised when working in a comfortable environment
— Curious and committed to lifelong learning
— Able to learn independently, seek out resources when stuck, and ask questions when needed
— Reflective and open to feedback

Limitations:

— Can feel overwhelmed when under pressure or when unclear about expectations or instructions
— Struggle with self-confidence when comparing myself to others
— I’m easily distracted in noisy or unstructured environments. I learn best in calm, quiet settings and may lose focus in loud surroundings I'm afraid and overwhelmed by thoughts of making a mistake by giving up my current career for something unknown, though I find it interesting

Non-technical skills I want to develop during my time at Dev Academy

While at Dev Academy, I would like to develop these crucial for developer human skills:
1. Communications.
I want to improve my ability to communicate with people who have different mindsets. It’s important to me to learn how to adapt to different communication styles while still staying myself. I also believe that effective, respectful communication is essential for any team to work well together.
2. Prioritising.
When there’s a lot to do, I can get overwhelmed and not know where to start. I want to learn how to focus on the most important tasks first and prioritise tasks.
3. Creativity.
I’d like to develop my creative thinking. Problem-solving in tech is often about finding new ways to approach old problems, and I want to train myself to think outside the box and not be afraid of trying something unusual.

My commitment to managing workload during The programme

I commit to managing my workload by staying organised, setting clear daily and weekly goals, and breaking down tasks into smaller, manageable steps. I’ll use tools like Toggle, to-do lists, or task boards to stay on track. I’ll also make sure to take regular breaks and maintain healthy boundaries to prevent burnout.

To work productively and safely with others, I’ll communicate openly and respectfully, listen actively, and be mindful of different mindsets and working styles. I’ll aim to contribute equally in group tasks and stay accountable for my part.

When I start to feel overwhelmed or notice stress building up, I’ll pause, take a short break, and reassess my priorities. I’ll ask for support if I need it and try to identify what exactly is causing the pressure. I’ll do my best to avoid burnout and keep learning effectively

My commitment to seeking help promptly and effectively

If I feel stuck or unsure about something, I commit to seeking help within 20–30 minutes rather than losing too much time trying to figure it out alone. I’ll first try to find answers by checking course resources, documentation, or my notes. If that doesn’t help, I’ll go and Google my questions and try to find answers online. And if that doesn’t help as well, I’ll reach out through the appropriate channels on Discord or ask a facilitator directly. I understand that seeking help is part of the learning process and not a weakness, and I’ll make an effort to be proactive and clear when looking for support.

What I expect from The Facilitation Team

I expect from Facilitation team to:

— Provide a safe, inclusive, and encouraging learning environment
— Communicate clearly about expectations and deadlines
— Offer timely, constructive feedback on my progress
— Be approachable and available for support when needed
— Show good human skills like active listening, empathy, and collaboration

Scheduling information and other commitments during The Programme

Weekday availability:
I’m generally available from 9:00am to 10:00pm, Monday to Friday.
Block-out times:
Weekends: Reserved for personal time and rest
Occasional appointments: I may have personal appointments during working hours on weekdays. I’ll communicate these in advance.

Bootcamp Learning Plan

What is my long term goal and career pathway?

My long-term goal is to build a career in software development, most likely in backend or full-stack development. At this stage, I’m not completely sure which stack or area I’ll enjoy the most, which is why I see this bootcamp as an opportunity to explore different technologies, build real projects, and discover what truly interests me. My main goal is to gain the practical skills and confidence to find my first job in the tech industry. From there, I plan to continue learning on the job, grow into a more specialised role over time, and contribute to meaningful projects. Ultimately, I want a career that challenges me intellectually and gives me room to grow.

What are my biggest strengths and limitations in Bootcamp?

My biggest strength is persistence. I keep going even when things are tough, and I enjoy problem-solving. During Foundations, I also improved my time management and learned to break tasks into smaller steps.
My main limitation is self-doubt. I sometimes overthink or hesitate when facing unfamiliar concepts. This can slow me down, but I’m learning to focus on progress rather than perfection.
I believe my perseverance and growing confidence will help me stay on track and get the most out of Bootcamp.

What is my biggest non-technical challenge at Bootcamp?

I think my biggest non-technical challenge will be staying focused and motivated throughout the full nine weeks of intense learning. I’m really interested in the material, but I know it can be mentally exhausting to keep up that level of concentration every day, especially when there’s a lot to take in at once.
To handle this, I’ll try to keep a good routine, take regular breaks, and make sure I have some time to rest outside of Bootcamp. I’ll also try not to get overwhelmed by everything at once and just take it step by step.

What non-technical skills - human skills - I would like to see developed in myself while at Dev Academy?

During Dev Academy, I’d like to work on communicating more confidently with different types of people. I sometimes find it challenging to express myself clearly when working with people who think or communicate differently, so learning how to adapt my communication style is something I want to improve.
I also want to develop some leadership skills, it's also important to me. I'd like to learn how to be able to guide or support others when needed and stay calm under pressure.
Lastly, I’d like to keep improving my self-awareness and reflective skills. I'd like to be able to recognise how I’m feeling, what’s working or not, and how I affect others. It is really valuable not just in tech, but in life in general.

What are my expectations from the Bootcamp team?

I’d like clear guidance on what’s expected each week so I can stay on track and not feel lost. I also hope the Bootcamp team will be supportive and easy to talk to. Someone I can go to with questions, even if they seem basic.
For me, support means knowing I can ask for help when I’m stuck, get honest feedback, and not feel judged. Just having someone to point me in the right direction when things get overwhelming is very important.

What are my expectations of myself on Bootcamp

I expect myself to stay committed, show up each day, and keep pushing through challenges even when it’s hard. I’ll try to solve problems on my own first, but if I’m stuck, I won’t wait too long to ask for help from peers, facilitators, or on Discord.
I’ll break things into small steps, stay organised, and take breaks to stay focused. I’ll communicate clearly, be respectful, and aim to create a safe, supportive space for myself and others.
