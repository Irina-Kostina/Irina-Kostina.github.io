Foundations Reflection

Throughout the Foundations course, the Human Skills learning has helped me understand myself better, both as a learner and as someone preparing to work in the tech industry. Using insights from my notes and the main ideas covered in materials, I’ve reflected on the key questions below.

What are three new things I have learnt about myself and my ego due to the Human Skills learning?

  1. I can choose how I respond in difficult moments.
    Before, I didn’t always realise I had a choice in how I reacted to frustration or stress. Learning about self-awareness and emotional control showed me that with practice, I can pause and respond more thoughtfully instead of just reacting.

  2. I sometimes take things too personally.
    If someone says something in a certain tone or acts differently, I might start wondering if I’ve done something wrong. Most of the time it has nothing to do with me, but I still take it to heart. Now, I try to pause and look at the situation more calmly.

  3. My ego prefers comfort zones.
    I’ve learnt that I tend to avoid situations that feel unfamiliar or where I might fail. This comes from a part of me wanting to stay safe and not risk looking unprepared. Becoming aware of this helped me understand that growth often requires discomfort.

What are the role of values, empathy, and self-awareness in learning and programming?

Values, empathy, and self-awareness play a much bigger role in learning and programming than I first expected.
Values help guide the way we work. Things like honesty, curiosity, and persistence keep us focused and motivated, especially when learning gets tough.
Empathy is important when working with others. Like in group projects or when thinking about the user experience. Being able to understand someone else’s point of view helps build better products and better teams.
Self-awareness helps us notice how we’re feeling and reacting. When for instanse we’re stuck on a bug or frustrated with slow progress. It makes it easier to manage emotions, ask for help when needed, and keep improving without getting overwhelmed. Together, these skills make learning and programming more human, more collaborative, and more sustainable in the long run.

What has surprised me the most about the Human Skills learning.

To be honest, nothing really surprised me. I was already aware of how important Human Skills are. And not just in tech, but in every field. This course simply confirmed that for me. It reminded me how much emotional intelligence, communication, and self-awareness impact the way we work and interact with others. I plan to keep developing these skills because I truly believe they’re just as important as technical knowledge. I also wish more people understood this. If they did, we’d have more productive meetings, healthier work environments, and stronger, more effective teams.

What were the most challenging aspects of the Human Skills learning?

One of the most challenging parts was slowing down and really reflecting on myself. It’s not always easy to look at your own behaviour or admit when something could be improved.

Why do I think DevAcademy are spending so much time focusing on Human Skills learning in a web development Bootcamp course?

Coding isn’t just about writing good code, it’s also about working well with people. Like in any real jobs, developers also need to communicate clearly, handle feedback, work in teams, manage stress, and stay motivated through challenges. Human Skills help with all of that. You can be the best programmer, but if you can’t collaborate or listen, it’s going to hold you back. By learning these skills we’re preparing to be strong and reliable team members in the tech industry.

Do I think the time I spent studying Human Skills learning feel like a waste of time?

No, I don’t think it was a waste of time. In fact, switching between technical studies and Human Skills was a good practice. It helped me balance different types of thinking. Logical problem-solving with emotional awareness and reflection. That mix made the learning process feel more complete and realistic, like how it would be in a real job. Human Skills supported my coding journey by helping me manage stress and stay motivated. So it definitely added value and made me more prepared for real-world work.
